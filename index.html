    1 Speech-to-text

    Where your voice becomes text.

    • On this computer: the audio never leaves the machine. Nothing downloads until you press DOWNLOAD on a model row.
    • Cloud: the audio is sent to your provider and transcribed there, so it needs an API key.
    • A missing model or a failed engine shows an error. SmoothFlow never quietly switches to the cloud instead.

    2 AI cleanup

    Optional. Rewrites what you said into clean written text.

    • On: after your words are written down, a second model tidies the sentence — commas, capital letters, half-finished corrections.
    • Off: you still get the correct words plus the built-in tidy-up rules. Only the extra rewriting is skipped.
    • A model on this computer needs a bigger download, more memory and a few extra seconds per dictation. A cloud model needs a provider and key instead.
