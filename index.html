    Cleanup
    Off by default. It improves punctuation and phrasing but adds several seconds and RAM.
    • On: after your words are written down, a second model tidies the sentence — commas, capital letters, half-finished corrections.
    • Off: you still get correct words, plus the built-in tidy-up rules. Only the extra rewriting is skipped.
    • It runs on this computer and needs a bigger download, more memory, and a few extra seconds per dictation.
